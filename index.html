About Pandit Ji

The Okanagan's own Hindu priest

For families across Kelowna and the Okanagan, finding a priest used to mean calling Vancouver or Calgary and hoping the dates lined up. Kelowna Hindu Priest serves the valley directly — Vedic puja, yagna and havan, griha pravesh, navagraha shanti and more, performed at your home.

Ceremonies are performed in the full Vedic tradition, with each step explained so everyone in the family — including the kids raised here — understands what's happening and why it matters.

Common questions

Before you book

Which areas do you serve?

Kelowna and West Kelowna primarily, with ceremonies across the Okanagan — Lake Country, Vernon and Penticton included. Mention your location when booking and travel can be confirmed.

Do I need to arrange the puja samagri (materials)?

After you book, you'll receive a simple samagri list for your ceremony. Most items are available locally, and anything hard to find can be discussed on WhatsApp beforehand.

Can you help choose an auspicious date (muhurat)?

Yes. Share the occasion and your flexible dates, and Pandit Ji can suggest a shubh muhurat that works for your family's schedule.

How far in advance should I book?

A week or two of notice is ideal, especially around festival season. For a vahan puja or a smaller blessing, same-week requests are often possible — just ask.
