FAQ / before you book

04 questions

Which areas do you serve? +

Kelowna and West Kelowna primarily, with ceremonies across the Okanagan — Lake Country, Vernon and Penticton included. Mention your location in the booking slip and travel can be confirmed.

Do I need to arrange the puja samagri? +

After you book you'll receive a simple samagri list for your ceremony. Most items are available locally, and anything hard to find can be sorted out on WhatsApp beforehand.

Can you help choose an auspicious date? +

Yes — share the occasion and your flexible dates, and Pandit Ji can suggest a shubh muhurat that works with your family's schedule. There's a “flexible” option right in the booking slip.

How far ahead should I book? +

A week or two is ideal, especially around festival season. Smaller blessings like a vahan puja can often happen the same week — just ask.
