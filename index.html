Before you book

Questions

A few practical details before sending your WhatsApp request.

Which areas do you serve? +

Kelowna, Penticton and Vernon are served, along with families across the Okanagan Valley. Mention your city in the request and travel can be confirmed.

Can the ceremony be at home or temple? +

Yes. Services can be performed at your home or at mandir according to your comfort. Temple locations are available in Kelowna and Summerland.

Do I need to arrange the puja samagri? +

After you book you will receive a simple samagri list for your ceremony. Anything hard to find can be sorted out on WhatsApp beforehand.

Can you help choose an auspicious date? +

Yes. Share the occasion and any flexible dates, and Pandit Ji can suggest a shubh muhurat that works with your family schedule.
